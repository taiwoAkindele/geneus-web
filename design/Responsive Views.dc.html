CareBridge — responsive behaviour

The same screens on tablet & desktop

Phone is the primary device, so it stays single-column with a bottom nav. On wider screens the bottom nav becomes a persistent left sidebar, and screens that were a drill-down (tap a patient → new page) become master–detail: the list and the open record sit side by side. Nothing is redesigned — the same components just reflow into the extra space.

≤ 640px · PHONE
Single column. Bottom tab bar. Full-screen forms & sheets. Everything one thumb can reach.
641–1024px · TABLET
Rail sidebar (icons + labels). Two-column content. Sheets become centered dialogs. Great in the consulting room.
≥ 1025px · DESKTOP
Full sidebar + master–detail. The records desk & admin live here — more visible at once, less navigating.

Desktop & tablet — onboarding (centered card on a brand split)

Onboarding screens (magic link, facility registration, create PIN, login) don't need master–detail — they're single tasks. So instead of stretching, the full-screen phone form becomes a fixed-width card centered on a branded split: a green welcome panel on the left, the form on the right. On tablet the panel narrows; below 640px the panel drops away and the card goes full-screen — exactly the phone screens in the library.
