One patient. One record. Every level of care.

An offline-first health record system built for the Community Health Centre first — simple enough for one nurse and unreliable power, and it grows upward into hospitals and the national health system.

05

Clinical records — history, new record & follow-up

When a referred patient reaches the doctor, their earlier visits are already there. The doctor reads the history, then either continues an existing episode as a follow-up — filed under the earlier visit — or starts a fresh new visit. The history screen (5.1) is live — tap a visit to expand it, and use the Tweaks panel to reshape the view; “New record” opens the picker that leads to the two record forms (5.2, 5.3), where complaints, the doctor's diagnosis, an auto-stamped date & time, treatment and prescription are all captured. This replaces the old standalone visit-note screen.
