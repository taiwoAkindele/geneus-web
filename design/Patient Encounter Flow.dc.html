How this flow works
One encounter, one screen
The whole visit is a single record, split into titled sections — vitals, complaint, lab order, lab results, diagnosis, dispensing, follow-up. Anyone viewing has the full picture at a glance.
Any staff, any field
There are no role locks — whoever is at the station records the step. Use the Signed in switch (top-right) to change who is saving; the save is stamped to that person.
Review, then lock
Saving always opens a summary of exactly what you entered. Confirm and the section locks — signed, timestamped, immutable. A mistake is fixed with an amendment, never an edit.
Who is at the workstation?
Saves are stamped to the signed-in staff member. Any of them can record any section.
